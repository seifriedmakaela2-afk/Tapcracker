# Tapcracker

TAPCRACKER is an interactive 3D tap shoe simulator that turns tap dancing into a digital musical instrument. Built with HTML, it lets users play virtual tap shoes using the keyboard, on-screen controls, or direct clicks on the shoes’ metal toe and heel plates. 

The simulator shows two realistic tap shoes on a virtual stage. The shoes are leather with soles, metal tap plates, screws, laces, and stitching. Users can rotate, pan, and zoom around the scene to view the shoes from different angles. Taps can be triggered with "Q" for the left toe, "A" for the left heel, "W" for the right toe, and "S" for the right heel. Users can also click directly on the tap plates or use the on-screen buttons. Each tap produces its own synthesized sound and visual feedback, including flashing plates, sparks, ripples, and shoe movement.

TAPCRACKER uses the Web Audio API to generate sound in real time, so no external audio files are needed. It includes four sound presets: Classic, Electric, 808, and Jazz. Users can also turn on three audio effects: distortion, reverb, and delay. In addition, the simulator includes five built-in rhythm patterns: Shuffle, Time Step, Paradiddle, Shim Sham, and WOW Demo. These patterns automatically play sequences of toe and heel taps at different tempos. The patterns are also very popular in the tap community.

Other features include a live audio visualizer with frequency bars and a waveform display, a velocity meter that reacts to tap intensity, a total tap counter, and a BPM estimate based on recent taps. There’s also an X-Ray mode that reveals a conceptual look inside the shoes, showing components like pressure sensors, wiring, a microcontroller, a battery, and a Bluetooth antenna. These are shown as 3D models to help imagine how electronic tap shoes might be built.
